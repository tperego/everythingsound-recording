Level and time differences are for an instrument at the edge of the ensemble. About 15 dB, or about 1.1 ms, or a mix of the two, puts a sound fully in one speaker.

About this simulator

It follows Michael Williams' method: each pair turns direction into a level difference (from the pattern and the angle) and a time difference (from the spacing), and the two add up. It is a simplified model, so treat the numbers as a guide, then listen. Read Williams' paper The Stereophonic Zoom, see his graphs at williamsmmad.com, and hear real recordings of each pair in MARRS, the University of Huddersfield's free app (Hyunkook Lee).
